# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our project a welcoming and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:
- Being respectful and considerate.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Welcoming different viewpoints and experiences.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, discrimination, and personal attacks.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards and may take appropriate corrective action.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and whenever an individual represents the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers. Reports will be reviewed and handled appropriately.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers may issue warnings, temporarily suspend participation, or remove a contributor from the project depending on the severity of the behavior.
